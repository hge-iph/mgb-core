# Project History and Contributors

MGB originated in Walter Collischonn's doctoral research on large-basin
hydrological simulation at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul in
2001. It was subsequently developed for research and operational applications
by the Large Scale Hydrology Research Group at the Institute of Hydraulic
Research (IPH/UFRGS).
